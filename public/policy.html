Section 3

What "FieLink Verified" means — and what it does not

This is the most important section in this document. Read it fully.

When you see the ✓ FieLink Verified badge on a listing, it means a FieLink representative has completed all of the following checks at the time of verification:

🏠
Physical site visit
A FieLink representative visited the property in person before the listing was published.
📸
Original photos
All photos were taken on-site by FieLink. No stock images or photos from other listings are used.
🪪
Landlord identity check
The landlord or authorised agent presented a valid Ghana Card or passport, which was recorded by FieLink.
📄
Ownership or authority docs
The landlord provided a title deed, lease agreement, or written authorisation confirming their right to let the property.
📱
Contact confirmation
The listed phone number was confirmed as active and reachable by WhatsApp at the time of verification.
🔄
Availability monitoring
FieLink makes reasonable efforts to remove listings within 24 hours of being informed a property is no longer available.

What the Verified badge does not guarantee:

! The Verified badge is a snapshot, not a guarantee

Our verification is conducted at a specific point in time. Property conditions, availability, price, terms, and ownership can change after verification. The badge confirms what we found on the date of our visit — it does not guarantee the same conditions will exist when you view or rent the property.

! We are not legal advisors on title

We review the documents presented to us in good faith. We are not a land registry, a court, or a legal authority. We cannot guarantee that a title deed is free from disputes, encumbrances, or competing claims. For any property purchase, you should independently engage a licensed Ghanaian solicitor to conduct a full title search.

! We do not guarantee the landlord's conduct

Verifying a landlord's identity does not make FieLink responsible for their behaviour during or after a tenancy. If a landlord misrepresents terms, withholds a deposit unlawfully, or breaches a rental agreement, FieLink is not liable for that conduct. Our dispute process (Section 9) is available to report misconduct.

! Unverified listings are clearly labelled

Some listings on the Platform may not carry the Verified badge. These are listed for information purposes only. FieLink takes no responsibility whatsoever for the accuracy or safety of unverified listings. We strongly recommend that users deal only with ✓ FieLink Verified listings.

All rental and purchase agreements are made directly between the Property Seeker and the Landlord or Agent. FieLink is not a party to any such agreement and accepts no liability for any loss, damage, fraud, or dispute arising from a transaction between users.

4.2 No liability for financial loss from third-party fraud

If a user suffers financial loss as a result of fraud committed by a landlord, agent, or any other third party found through the Platform — including in cases where the listing carried the Verified badge — FieLink's liability is limited to removal of the fraudulent listing and reporting assistance as described in Section 9. FieLink does not provide financial compensation for losses arising from third-party fraud.

4.3 No liability for property condition

FieLink does not inspect properties on behalf of tenants for structural safety, habitability, or legal compliance with building regulations. Any assessment of property condition is the sole responsibility of the Property Seeker prior to entering a rental agreement.

4.4 Platform availability

4.5 Limitation of liability

Section 5

Property seeker responsibilities

By using FieLink to search for property, you agree to the following.

5.1 Conduct your own due diligence

Before paying any deposit, advance rent, or fee to a landlord or agent, you should physically visit the property, verify the landlord's identity in person, review any tenancy agreement carefully, and consider obtaining independent legal advice for high-value transactions.

5.2 Never pay before viewing

FieLink strongly advises against making any payment — including deposits, "holding fees", or advance rent — before you have physically visited the property and confirmed that the person you are dealing with is the verified landlord or their authorised agent.

5.3 Insist on a written tenancy agreement

Any rental agreement should be documented in writing before money changes hands. The Ghana Rent Act requires a written tenancy agreement for any rental exceeding six months. FieLink recommends using a written agreement for all tenancies regardless of duration.

5.4 Report suspicious listings immediately

If you encounter a listing you believe to be fraudulent, misrepresented, or duplicated from another platform, report it to FieLink immediately at report@fielink.com or via WhatsApp. Do not pay the listed party.

Section 6

Landlord and agent responsibilities

6.1 Accuracy of information

By listing a property on FieLink, you warrant that all information provided — including photos, description, price, location, and availability — is accurate and not misleading at the time of listing. You agree to inform FieLink within 24 hours if a listed property is no longer available.

6.2 Authority to list

You confirm that you are the lawful owner of the listed property, or that you are a licensed agent with written authorisation from the owner to market and let the property on their behalf. Listing a property you do not have authority to let is a breach of this policy and may constitute fraud under Ghanaian law.

6.3 Verification cooperation

You agree to cooperate fully with FieLink's verification process, including providing valid identification, allowing a FieLink representative to visit and photograph the property, and supplying relevant ownership or authorisation documents.

6.4 Verification fees are non-refundable

Verification fees paid to FieLink cover the cost of the physical site visit and documentation process. Fees are non-refundable once the verification visit has been conducted, regardless of whether the listing is subsequently published.

6.5 Consequences of providing false information

If FieLink discovers that a landlord or agent has provided false or misleading information — including forged documents, misrepresented photos, or false identity — the listing will be immediately removed, the user will be permanently banned from the Platform, and FieLink reserves the right to report the matter to the Ghana Police Service and relevant authorities.

Section 7

Prohibited conduct

The following actions are strictly prohibited on the FieLink Platform and may result in immediate account removal, reporting to authorities, and legal action:

7.1 Fraudulent listings

Listing a property you do not own or have no authority to let; using photographs copied from other platforms or listings; advertising a property that has already been let or does not exist as described.

7.2 Identity misrepresentation

Presenting false identity documents during the verification process, or impersonating another person, landlord, or agent.

7.3 Double-listing and double-letting

Listing the same property on multiple platforms simultaneously with the intent to collect deposits from multiple prospective tenants.

7.4 Demanding fees not agreed in advance

Charging property seekers undisclosed fees, "viewing fees", or agent commissions that were not clearly stated in the listing.

7.5 Harassment or threatening conduct

Using the Platform to harass, threaten, or intimidate any other user, FieLink representative, or third party.

Section 9

Dispute and complaints process

If you have a complaint about a listing, a landlord, an agent, or our verification process, follow these steps.

  • Report to FieLink immediately. Email report@fielink.com or WhatsApp us at [INSERT NUMBER]. Include the listing link or ID, your name and contact number, and a clear description of the issue.

  • We acknowledge within 24 hours. FieLink will confirm receipt of your complaint within 24 hours (business days) and assign a reference number.

  • Investigation within 5 business days. We will contact the relevant landlord or agent and review evidence provided by both parties. If a listing is found to be fraudulent or misleading, it will be removed immediately upon confirmation — not at the end of the process.

  • Outcome communicated within 10 business days. We will inform you of our findings and any action taken. If the complaint is upheld, we will provide a written summary you can use for any further legal action or police report.

  • Escalation. If you are unsatisfied with our resolution, you may escalate to the Ghana Data Protection Commission (for data-related complaints at compliance@dataprotection.org.gh), the Ghana Police Service (for fraud), or the Rent Control Department (for tenancy disputes). FieLink will cooperate fully with any official investigation.

! FieLink cannot recover lost funds

We do not hold funds on behalf of users and cannot recover money paid to a landlord or agent. If you have been defrauded, report to the Ghana Police Service CID immediately. FieLink will provide your complaint record and any landlord identification documents we hold to support an official police investigation upon receipt of a formal request.
