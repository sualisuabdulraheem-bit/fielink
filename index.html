The FieLink Verification Standard
This isn't a badge we hand out. It's a 6-step process enforced by our own admin software — a listing cannot go live until every step is complete.

Physical site visit

A FieLink representative visits and walks through every property before it's listed. No exceptions.

Original photos only

All photos are taken on-site by FieLink. No stock images. No photos reused from other listings.

Identity verified

The landlord presents a valid Ghana Card or passport, checked and recorded before listing.

Ownership documents

Title deed, lease, or written letting authority is reviewed before the listing is published.

Contact confirmed

The listed phone number is confirmed active and reachable on WhatsApp at time of publishing.

Live availability

Listings are removed within 24 hours of being reported let. No ghost listings sitting for months.
