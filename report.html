Trust & Safety

Report a scam or suspicious listing

If something feels wrong — a listing that looks fake, a landlord asking for money before a viewing, or a property that doesn't match the photos — tell us immediately. We investigate every report.

If you've already lost money
FieLink cannot recover funds already paid to a third party. Report to the Ghana Police Service CID immediately. We will cooperate fully with any official investigation and can provide your report record on request.
